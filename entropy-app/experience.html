BEHIND THE NUMBERS

How it works.

A clearer view of the creators contributing to the Entropy conversation.

01 / DISCOVER

Find the conversation.

We index public X posts that mention @entropyIO. Replies and reposts are excluded.

02 / MEASURE

Follow the impact.

Views and engagement come from the latest available post metrics. Numbers may update at different times. Smart Followers counts are provided by TwitterScore (twitterscore.io).

03 / RANK

Creator Score.

Creators are ranked by total views across indexed posts.

Score combines views, weighted reactions, reach relative to audience size, and a capped Smart Followers bonus. Accepted posts add up to 20%. Engagement above 10% can reduce the score once a creator has over 1,000 views. The influence map ranks creators by views.

Coverage is partial. Views are cumulative and do not represent unique people or verified organic reach.
