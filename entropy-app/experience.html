        Creator Score

        The score combines impressions and reactions across a creator’s counted posts. Campaign rankings use the same formula with the posts accepted for each week.

        Base score× ER multiplier× Post multiplier× Anti-farm

        Base score components

        Audience quality
        Up to 540 points
        Smart Followers from TwitterScore earn a logarithmic bonus, capped at 35% of the reach points.
        Reach
        10 × √impressions
        Impressions add points at a diminishing rate, so each additional thousand contributes less as reach grows.
        Engagement depth
        1 / 3 / 4–6
        A like counts for 1, a repost for 3, and a quote for 4 to 6 depending on reach. Weighted reactions are capped at 1% to 5% of impressions.
        Reach efficiency
        Up to 240 points
        Impressions earn a bonus in proportion to how far they reach relative to the creator’s follower count, with diminishing returns.

        Dynamic multipliers

        Engagement rate
        0.90× to 1.00×
        Likes, reposts, and quotes divided by impressions give the engagement rate. The multiplier reaches its maximum at 5%.
        Posting consistency
        +1% per post
        Each counted post adds one percentage point to the multiplier. The bonus tops out at 20 posts.
        Anti-farm adjustment
        Up to −35%
        The penalty begins above 10% engagement and 1,000 impressions. It reaches its maximum at 20% engagement and 5,000 impressions.

        Full formula

        V
        Impressions
        L, R, Q
        Likes, reposts, quotes
        F
        Total followers
        SF
        Smart Followers
        P
        Counted posts

        Base score

        Scale = min(1, V / 20,000)
Reach = 10 × √V
Audience = min(540, 0.35 × Reach)
           × min(1, log₁₀(1 + SF) / 3)
Reactions = L + 3 × R + (4 + 2 × Scale) × Q
Cap = V × (0.01 + 0.04 × Scale)
Engagement = 0.7 × min(Reactions, Cap) × Scale^1.5
Efficiency = 120 × min(ln(1 + V / max(F, 1)), 2) × Scale
Base = Reach + Audience + Engagement + Efficiency

        Multipliers and result

        ER = (L + R + Q) / V
ER multiplier = 0.90 + 2 × min(ER, 0.05)
Post multiplier = 1 + 0.01 × min(P, 20)
Anti-farm = 1 − 0.35 × clamp((ER − 0.10) / 0.10)
                     × clamp((V − 1,000) / 4,000)

Score = Base × ER multiplier × Post multiplier × Anti-farm

        clamp(x) keeps a value between 0 and 1. If Smart Followers or total followers are unavailable, the corresponding bonus is zero. The leaderboard displays the score rounded to a whole number.
